I'm excited to be joining South Park Commons NYC as a resident member. I'm here to answer one question: how can we make the power of quantum computing accessible to everyone who can benefit from it?

We are entering a post-NISQ, AI-native world, where the capabilities of quantum computers, as well as the tools with which we can program them, are rapidly advancing. Frontier AI tools are now at the fingertips of anyone in the world. How can we enable the power of quantum to become equally ubiquitous?

I'm grateful to SPC for the opportunity to join this unique community of builders and founders. Stay tuned for updates over the coming months.
