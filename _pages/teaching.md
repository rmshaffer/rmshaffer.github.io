I teach and mentor whenever I have the opportunity. Helping more people learn to program quantum computers is one of the most direct ways I know to make quantum computing accessible to everyone who can benefit from it.

## New York University

**Adjunct Professor, [Department of Applied Physics](https://engineering.nyu.edu/academics/departments/applied-physics), NYU Tandon School of Engineering** (2026–present)

I teach the undergraduate and graduate courses in quantum programming:

- **PH-UY 2012: Introduction to Quantum Programming.** An accessible introduction for undergraduates: the fundamental quantum algorithms, and how to implement them with today's quantum programming languages and frameworks.
- **PH-GY 6623: Quantum Programming.** A rigorous, hands-on graduate course in building, analyzing and running quantum circuits and algorithms, using frameworks such as Qiskit and PennyLane.
